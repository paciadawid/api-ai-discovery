UC-CARTWS-12A product moved to the wishlist leaves the cart, and moving it back returns it under new line ids
open product pagePRODUCT→ has 1 chronographADD→ move to wishlistMOVE→ read cart, wishlist, countersCART, WISHLIST, COUNTERS→ move back to cartMOVE→ read cart, wishlist, countersCART, WISHLIST, COUNTERS

Product page

  • HTTP 200, offers add-to-cart, price matches $24,110.00 format
  • The product id in its add-to-cart link is the chronograph's (1)

Move to the wishlist

  • MOVE accepted with wasMoved:true
  • CART is empty; the wishlist has the chronograph at quantity 1 under a new line id
  • COUNTERS: cart 0, wishlist 1

Move back

  • MOVE accepted with wasMoved:true
  • The cart line is back at quantity 1 with yet another new id
  • The wishlist is empty; COUNTERS: cart 1, wishlist 0

Not covered

  • Only simple products, and only the golfball and the chronograph.
  • Stock and maximum-quantity limits, coupons, shipping and tax estimation.
  • Logged-in carts (the customer fixture exists but no cart test uses it).
  • Carts surviving a session change (guest cart merging into the customer cart after login).
Every test gets its own shopper with a private cart (unique User-Agent). The fixture empties the cart and wishlist afterwards, even when a test fails.

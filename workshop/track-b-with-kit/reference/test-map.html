Given (setup) When (behaviour under test) Then (observation) Every test gets its own visitor: a unique User-Agent, because the host keys a guest cart by IP + User-Agent.

CART-05Quantity 0 answers HTTP 500 although the cart WAS changed@known-issue

CART-06A visitor's cart is private; another visitor cannot see or delete its lines
shopper adds 1 golfballADD→other adds 2 golfballsADD→other tries to delete shopper's lineDELETE→read both cartsCART→other deletes OWN lineDELETE→read other cartCART
  • The two visitors got different line ids; the other cart never lists mine
  • My cart is untouched: 1 line, same id, quantity 1; their line is untouched too (quantity 2)
  • Positive control: deleting a line you own DOES work. Without it, "my cart is untouched" also holds when delete does nothing at all (see mutant 1 in the first draft)

Can these tests fail? (sabotage)

bash scripts/sabotage.sh reference cart.spec.ts breaks the helper code one way at a time and runs the suite.

What no test checks yet

Two different products in one cart (mutant 4). Every test has at most one line per cart, so a price reader that is right for line 1 and wrong for line 2 goes unnoticed. The test that closes the hole is reference/cart-07-kills-mutant-4.snippet.txt: 2 golfballs ($1.90) and 1 chronograph ($24,110.00) in the same cart, each line with its own quantity and price, subtotal $24,113.80 (2 × $1.90 + $24,110.00), badge 3.
  • Quantity boundaries other than 0: negative, very large, non-numeric (the host answers 502 for non-numeric).
  • Update or delete of one line while a second line exists.
  • Logged-in carts, stock limits, coupons, shipping and tax.

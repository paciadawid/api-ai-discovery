# Block 7: the challenge

**Goal:** the whole day once more, on an API you have not seen, with no card to lead you.

Read `workshop/challenge.md`, pick **one tier**, and go. The first step is yours: **explore, find gaps, plan, then code.**

| Minutes | What | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Explore: prompt 07 (steps 1 to 3), or by hand in Postman | `qa/<api>-gaps.md` with evidence |
| 10 | Plan: four to six tests with sources, a plan **before** code | `qa/test-plan.md` (new section) |
| 20 | Code: three or four tests, one at a time, cleanup in a fixture | green tests |
| 10 | Break one thing nobody should notice, then score yourself (10-point list in `challenge.md`) | your score |

- **First API of your life?** Tier A (Pokémon) first. Tier B (the cart) is the richer one.
- **Done early?** Do the other tier, or help a neighbour who is stuck.
- No hints for the first 10 minutes. After that: the hints in `challenge.md`.
- Careful with logins: never a wrong password for your own user (see `AGENTS.md`).

**Done when:** at least three tests are green, every one with a business assertion, and you have a score you can defend.
